Frequently asked questions

How remote dental CAD design works with PRODIGY Lab Dental from Bogotá, Colombia: files, turnaround, revisions, payment in USD and international shipping.

Services
Do you work with clinics and labs outside Colombia?
Yes. Remote CAD design reaches any country: you upload the scan, we design it and you download the files. Prices are in USD and we answer on WhatsApp in English or Spanish. Finished veneers, implant crowns and full-arch work can also be shipped to you by international courier (see veneers, implant crowns and full arch).
What do I receive with a design-only order?
A universal STL ready to mill or print on your own equipment, compatible with any CAM system. We can also deliver native Exocad or 3Shape files, plus OBJ and PLY.
Which design software do you use?
Exocad and 3Shape, depending on the case. Our designers have clinical experience: they work occlusion, margins and esthetics case by case.
Which implant systems do you support?
We have libraries for Straumann, Nobel Biocare, Neodent, MegaGen, Osstem, Zimmer, Dentsply Sirona (Ankylos, Xive), BioHorizons, Alpha Bio, Cortex and more. If your system is not on the list, tell us and we can import the library within 24 hours.
Files
Which file formats do you accept?
STL, OBJ, PLY, native Exocad files (.constructionfile), 3Shape Communicate projects (.3oxz), exports from iTero, Medit i500/i700 and Primescan, and DICOM files for surgical guide planning.
How do I send my case?
Upload the scan in our online order form, share a Google Drive, WeTransfer or Dropbox link, or send it on WhatsApp at +57 321 281 6716.
What if my mill cannot open the STL?
We deliver universal STL files that work on any CAM system: Amann Girrbach, XTCERA, VHF, Roland, Sirona inLab, Zirkonzahn and others. If a file does not open, contact us and we fix it the same day at no charge.
Turnaround
How long does a design take?
Simple single units (crown, veneer): 15 minutes to 2 hours.
Bridges of 3 to 4 units: 2 to 4 hours.
Full arch or All-on-4: 4 to 8 hours.
Surgical guides: 4 to 8 hours plus CBCT planning.
These are business hours, Monday to Friday, 8 am to 5 pm Colombia time, and they start when payment is confirmed.
Can I send a case at night or on the weekend?
Yes. We receive cases 24/7.

Monday to Friday: 8 am to 5 pm Colombia time. A case that arrives before 5 pm starts that day; otherwise, the next business day.
Saturdays: closed to the public. We work behind closed doors, usually 9 am to 12 pm, receiving and shipping cases as the lab workload allows.

Anything received on a Saturday, Sunday or holiday is dated the following Monday (or Tuesday if that Monday is a Colombian holiday). Colombia is on UTC−5 all year, with no daylight saving time: the same as US Eastern in winter and US Central in summer.
Pricing & payment
How much does a design cost?
Each unit has a fixed price in USD. The design calculator shows the price of your case instantly, with no sign-up. For full-arch cases and surgical guides we confirm the quote on WhatsApp before we start.
How do I pay from outside Colombia?
PayPal (which also takes credit and debit cards), Wise or international wire transfer, in USD. International payments carry a 7% fee that covers the platform and currency exchange. We send you the PayPal payment link on WhatsApp. If you pay by transfer, send us the receipt on WhatsApp and we coordinate the details of your case there. Finished restorations shipped abroad are paid 50% to start and 50% before shipment (see our terms).
Do you do a free test case?
No. No service is free, including the first case: every job takes hours of skilled work, software, materials and review.

If you want to know whether your case is feasible before ordering, you can request a case assessment: we review your files and tell you what can be done, how long it takes and what it costs. Its fee depends on the case and the type of service (a crown is not the same as a surgical guide or an aligner plan); we confirm it on WhatsApp before starting, and the work starts once payment is confirmed.

To see our work, browse the portfolio of real cases.
Revisions & tracking
How many revisions are included?
Every design includes up to 2 revisions, included in the price, before final approval; request them within 48 hours of delivery. If you ask for more changes after receiving the second revision, each extra revision costs 20% of the design price. Nothing is charged upfront: only if you request it, and we remind you when you ask for the first and second revisions. A change of plan (a different material, shape or type of restoration) is quoted separately, according to the size of the change.
How do I track my case?
Enter your order code, for example CAD-7F3A9C21B4, at prodigylabdental.com/seguimiento-caso to see which stage your case is in. The code comes in the WhatsApp message you receive when you place the order.
Shipping & warranty
Do you ship finished restorations abroad?
Yes. Veneers, crowns and implant restorations ship by DHL, FedEx or UPS with tracking. Typical turnaround is 3 to 5 business days plus shipping. The ordering dentist is the importer of record and covers any import duties or taxes in the destination country.
What if a restoration does not fit?
If a restoration is defective because of a laboratory error (marginal fit, contacts, or a design that does not match the approved preview), we remake it at no charge. The warranty does not cover inadequate preparation, distorted or incomplete scans, bite-registration errors, incorrect shade selection, or clinical and patient factors.
