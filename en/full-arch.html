Frequently asked

Everything about
full-arch restorations

What is the difference between All-on-4 and All-on-6?
All-on-4 uses four implants (two axial and two tilted to avoid the sinus and nerve) for a fixed full-arch bridge; All-on-6 adds two implants to better distribute loads in long arches or lower bone density. We design and manufacture both — the framework, access channels and cantilever are planned to the number and angulation of implants.
Do you make the immediate-load provisional and the definitive hybrid?
Yes, both stages: an immediate-load PMMA provisional for the day of surgery and the definitive hybrid, monolithic in zirconia or on a titanium bar, once osseointegration is confirmed. We also design the single implant crowns and the surgical guide for the same case.
What do I need to send for a full-arch case?
An intraoral scan with scan bodies in the implant positions, the antagonist and a bite registration, plus the surgical plan if available. Extraoral photos help define the smile line and lip support.
Which implant systems and multi-unit abutments do you work with?
We work with connections and multi-unit abutments from 100+ brands (Straumann, Nobel Biocare, Neodent, BioHorizons, MIS, Osstem, Zimmer and more). Just tell us the brand, platform and type of multi-unit abutment.
How much is it and how fast?
It depends on the number of units, the material and the stage (provisional or definitive). Tell us the case on WhatsApp and we'll confirm exact pricing and timing. We work on a 50% to start, 50% on delivery basis.
