Two approaches

Screw-retained or cemented,
per implant

The same design precision, the approach that best serves your case. We decide it together before milling, based on implant angulation and esthetic demand.

Screw-retained

Crown + abutment in one piece (one-piece) or crown screwed onto an abutment. Retrievable, no residual cement and easy to remove for maintenance. Ideal when angulation allows or with an angulated screw channel.

Cemented

Crown on a custom abutment when anterior esthetics or implant angulation won't let the screw exit through the facial. Maximum esthetic freedom, with margins designed for controlled cementation.
